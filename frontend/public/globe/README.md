# Earth texture

`earth.jpg` is the 2048 × 1024 Earth texture distributed with the
[Three.js planet examples](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets),
downloaded unchanged from
[earth_atmos_2048.jpg](https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg).
The source imagery is NASA's
[Blue Marble](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/collections/blue-marble/).
Credit: NASA. The asset is served locally and does not contact a third-party
tile or imagery service at runtime.
